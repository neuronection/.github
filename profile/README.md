Four open-source AI assistants — health, career, study, desktop. One family,
one idea: structure your data, keep it yours, and let AI explain itself.

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Health Assistant](https://github.com/health-assistant-io/health-assistant) | Self-hosted, privacy-first health records |
| [Career Assistant](https://github.com/neuronection/career-assistant) | Career discovery for students |
| [Study Assistant](https://github.com/neuronection/study-assistant) | Local-first AI study workbench |
| [Desktop Assistant](https://github.com/neuronection/desktop-assistant) | AI launcher in your system tray |

All repos are Apache-2.0 and in beta.
Learn more at **[neuronection.com](https://neuronection.com)**.
